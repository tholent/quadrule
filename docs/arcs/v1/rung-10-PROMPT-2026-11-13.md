- **Budget:** rung 10 is "large" on the board. Rung 9 cost one builder plus one resumed leg; read its calibration row.
  The weekly meter resets Saturday 14th, 5 pm; after it the hard stops are 75 / 80 / 85 / 93 % again (the 90 % stop
  was this week only).
- **Speed:** a repeat multiplies objects, so a grid repeat of a heavy source is the new heaviest page. Build a repeat
  page and time dragging and drawing on it on the Pi (release build, both canvases, strip on, 90th percentile,
  ADR-009); say before anything crosses 50 ms. The chip canvas sat at 41 ms drawing on the pattern page.
- **Model:** a repeat is a **live** object (spec §6.4): editing the source updates every instance, and Expand turns it
  into plain objects. It touches groups (a source may be a group; ADR-015, groups see-through to constraints),
  per-device overrides (rung 8), snapping (pitch defaults to one rhythm unit; `u` values) and lint (L16 judges repeat
  spacing; L17 covers "repeat unbound from the rhythm", spec §8.3 line 449, so rung 9's L17 must learn repeats).
- **Questions a picture would settle:** rung 9's lesson is that a shape question answered in words can turn at the
  look. For layout questions in the bbq (alternation, fill to bounds, grid order), put a small sketch in the question.
- **Starters:** Paper, Notes and Creative are buildable now; chris designs one per category (board, chris's queue).
  Planning and Bullet journal become buildable after this rung.
