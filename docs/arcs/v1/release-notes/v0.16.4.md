**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
brings the second batch of fixes asked for before the starters: proper menus on every system, and double-clicking a
`.quad` on the Mac, approved by chris on 2026-11-23. Quick Look comes in `v0.16.5`, the starters in `v0.16.6`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New

- **Menus.** **File** (New…, Open…, Open Recent, Save, Save As…, Save as template…, Export…, Close), **Edit** (Undo,
  Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Select all), **View** (zoom, the device previews, Snapping, safe zones, theme,
  canvas) and **Help** (About quadrule, quadrule on GitHub). Each item shows its key, and is greyed when it cannot act.
- **On Windows and Linux** the menus sit along the top of the window.
- **On the Mac** they are in the menu bar at the top of the screen, with ⌘ keys, after a **Quadrule** menu (About,
  Settings… which opens Profiles, Hide, Hide Others, Show All, Quit) and with a **Window** menu. Quitting with unsaved
  changes asks first.
- **Double-click a `.quad` in Finder** to open it, whether quadrule is closed or already open (it asks first if the
  open page has unsaved changes). If several are opened at once, the first opens.
- **Edit ▸ Select all** picks every visible, unlocked object.

## Changed

- **The snapping switches** moved from the status bar to **View ▸ Snapping**; the status bar keeps the pointer's
  position, the zoom and the warnings count.
- **The toolbar's View button and File button are gone**; what they held is in the View and File menus.
- **Esc** that closes an open menu now keeps your selection (it used to clear it too).
- **Redo** is shown as ⇧⌘Z on the Mac and Ctrl+Y elsewhere; both keys work on every system.

## Files

- No change to the `.quad` format (still **0.18.0**) or to exports.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a, borders, layers and fixes batches 1 and 2)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff and grand staff) with optional borders and rounded corners, turned whole or with their lines turned
  inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, smooth zoom, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery; menus on every system.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.16.4`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.16.3`.
