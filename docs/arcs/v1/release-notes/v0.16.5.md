**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
brings what the first starters needed: pages that run to the edge like paper, a Margin role, quieter lint on drawing
grids, and a calendar pattern and a mini calendar, approved by chris on 2026-11-23. The starters come in `v0.16.6`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New

- **A calendar pattern.** A pattern region can be a **Calendar**: a month of day cells under a row of weekday names,
  seven columns, a header row one rhythm unit tall and as many week rows as the month spans. Pick a **Month** and
  **Year** and every day is numbered in its top-right corner; leave it **Undated** and set where the **1st falls** and
  the **days in the month** (28 to 31), and each day gets a small empty square there to write its date in. Only the
  month's own days are boxed. **Week starts on** is Monday unless you change it. Weekday names are as long as the
  columns have room for: Monday, Mon or M.
- **A mini calendar.** The **Mini calendar** tool (M) places a small block of this month, weekday initials and day
  numbers under the month and year, anywhere on the page, such as a corner of a daily page. Its month, year, first
  day of the week and title are in the Style tab.
- A calendar's words shrink to fit, but never below the smallest size that reads on the device: when they would, they
  are left out and the linter's **L07** says so. The words are in English for now.
- **Full bleed.** A tick beside Free placement in the Arrange tab marks an object as meant to run under the tablet's
  toolbars and to the page edge, as paper does: the linter's safe-area (L06) and off-page (L10) rules leave it alone,
  and a marked group or repeat covers what is inside it.
- **A Margin role**, for wide, light lines such as a notebook's margin rule: in the Style tab, the Profiles window and
  the calibration sheet.

## Changed

- **Page edges count as on the rhythm**: an object flush with a page edge no longer gets L16's "off the rhythm".
- **L10** allows half a stroke past the page edge, as a line drawn on the edge always reaches.
- **Drawing grids are left alone**: L14 (soft thin lines) skips hex and isometric patterns, and L09 (handwriting step)
  skips an isometric rhythm.
- **L18** no longer says "-0 px".

## Files

- The `.quad` format is now **0.19.0**: files from earlier versions open unchanged; a file holding a calendar, a mini
  calendar, a Full bleed mark or the Margin role needs this version. Exports of pages without them are unchanged.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a, borders, layers, fixes batches 1 and 2, the starter fixes and calendars)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell, music
  staff and grand staff, calendar) with optional borders and rounded corners, turned whole or with their lines turned
  inside, and mini calendars; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, smooth zoom, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery; menus on every system.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.
