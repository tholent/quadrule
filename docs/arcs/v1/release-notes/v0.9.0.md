## New in rung 9: the pattern library

### New patterns

- **Isometric**: vertical lines with slanted lines at 30° and 150° crossing on them, the drafting-paper layout. Choose
  **lines** (the triangle grid) or **dots** (a dot at each crossing). The vertical lines stay pixel-sharp.
- **Hex**: a honeycomb with a **flat top** or a **pointy top**. A hexagon's height is a whole number of rhythm units.
- **Cornell**: ruled lines with a cue column on the left and a summary band at the bottom, both sized in rhythm units.
- **Music staff**: five-line staves with their own line gap (2 mm by default), one every few rhythm units. Tick
  **Grand staff** to pair them (treble and bass) with a joining line at the left.
- Lines and edges that cross the region's border are cut at the border, so nothing sticks out past it.

### Major lines

- **Major every N** in the Page tab marks every Nth rhythm line as major. Pattern regions follow it, and each region
  can switch major lines off or set its own N and role (Structure by default).
- Major dots are one pixel bigger than ordinary dots. The rhythm overlay draws major lines stronger.

### A region's own spacing

- Untick **Bound to rhythm** to give a pattern region its own spacing, in any unit (`u` included).
- **Lint L17 (mixed rhythm)** notes a region whose own spacing is not a clean multiple or fraction of the rhythm.

### The isometric rhythm

- The rhythm has a **Type**: Rectangular (as before) or **Isometric**, with one side length. Snapping, Shift+arrow and
  drawn sizes then step one column across and half a side down, so every isometric crossing can be caught. The overlay
  draws the triangle grid.
- New… has an **Isometric 5 mm** preset.

### Speed

- The graphics-chip canvas draws pattern regions faster: drawing one on the shapes page on a Raspberry Pi 5 went from
  about 51 ms to 41 ms a frame.

### Files

- Files are saved as format **0.9.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–9)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), from one design, in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes, and
  pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell, music staff), bound to the page's
  rectangular or isometric rhythm or spaced on their own; groups of any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and
  crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with nine rules.
