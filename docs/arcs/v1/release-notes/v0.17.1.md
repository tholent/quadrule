**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release builds
the tablets' grays on the Supernote pens' own grays, approved by chris on 2026-11-26.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

On a Mac with Homebrew, `brew install --cask tholent/tap/quadrule` installs the same app as the `.dmg`, once the tap
has been filled in for this release.

## Changed

- **Grays built on the pens.** The 16 grays offered for the Manta and the Nomad are now 1, 18, 36, 53, 70, 88, 105, 122,
  140, 157, 172, 186, 201, 219, 237 and 255. Three of them are the Supernote pens' own grays: the black pen (1), the
  dark grey pen (157) and the light grey pen (201); the page stays white at 255. Each new gray is within 4 of the one
  it replaces, so on the tablet's screen pages look the same as before.
- **The line and fill roles moved with them.** Labels are now exactly the dark grey pen's gray (157), and Margin
  lines and Shade fills the light grey pen's (201); the other roles moved by 1 to 3 levels. Paper sizes moved the same
  way.
- **The gray picker names the pens:** the Style tab's swatches for 1, 157 and 201 read "black pen", "dark grey pen"
  and "light grey pen" on hover, and the chosen one is named under the swatches.
- **One lint rule retired:** L04 ("gray close to pen ink"), never built, is dropped; a template gray may match a pen.
- Every exported PNG changes by a few gray levels.

## Files

- The `.quad` format is now **0.22.0**. A file from an earlier version opens with each hand-picked gray that sat on one
  of the old 16 grays moved to the new one in its place (153 becomes 157, 204 becomes 201); a gray picked off the old
  grays, and everything set by a role, stays as it was. Profiles you made or edited keep their own grays; Reset to
  built-in brings an edited Manta or Nomad onto the new ones.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–17)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with 20 starters, recent files and your own
  templates; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for
  eleven paper sizes at a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or
  landscape, named and foldered the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell, music
  staff and grand staff, calendar) with optional borders and rounded corners, major lines counted from where you
  choose, turned whole or with their lines turned inside, and mini calendars; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- A Basic tab with the few settings a template's designer chose to show.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, smooth zoom, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery; menus on every system, a keyboard
  shortcuts sheet, an interface size, and names for screen readers.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, on a gray scale built on the pens' grays, editable in the
  Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.17.1`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.17.0`.
