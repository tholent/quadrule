**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
marks rung 6a, approved by chris on 2026-11-11.

## Download

**No installers are attached to this release.** The repository's releases are immutable (files cannot be added
once a release is published), and this one was published before its packages were built. Installers return with
the next release; until then, v0.5.0 is the latest release with downloads, or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New in rung 6a: shapes

- **Three new tools:** Rectangle (R), Ellipse (E) and Polygon (Y). Drag to draw with snapping, or click for a default
  size; hold Shift for a square, a circle or a regular polygon. A star is a polygon with **Star** ticked.
- **Fill and stroke:** a shape can have a fill, a stroke, or both. Two new fill roles each device resolves to its own
  gray: **Shade** (a light tint for boxes and highlights) and **Block** (a dark solid, such as a header bar), plus a
  custom gray. Shade and Block start at estimates until they are measured on the devices.
- **Dashes and caps:** Dotted, Short dash, Dash and Long dash, or your own lengths, each landing on whole device
  pixels; butt, round or square ends. They work on lines too.
- **Shape settings:** corner radius (in any unit, rhythm units included), number of sides (3 to 32), and a star's
  inner radius, in a new Shape section of the inspector.
- Curved and slanted edges are smoothed; straight edges stay pixel-exact.
- The linter checks the new shapes' strokes (L01, L02) and safe zones (L06).
- File format 0.5.0: files saved by earlier versions open unchanged and export the same pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–6a)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872).
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars, text boxes, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid) bound to the page's rhythm.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with seven rules.

Source: `v0.6.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.5.0`.
