## New in rung 12a: missing fonts

- **A page opens even when its fonts are missing.** Every saved page keeps the outlines of its text for each tablet,
  so a page whose fonts this computer lacks opens at once, looking exactly as designed. A yellow bar above the page
  names the missing fonts.
- **Choose what to do per font.** The bar's **Choose…** button opens the Missing fonts dialog, with one row per font:
  - **Use saved outlines:** the text stays exactly as designed. You can still move, turn, fade, copy, group or delete
    it, but not change its words, font or size until a font is chosen. A badge in the Text tab and a ⚠ in the layers
    panel say why.
  - **Substitute:** quadrule suggests the closest installed or bundled font, matched by measuring each font's shape
    (serif, sans, mono or handwritten; weight, width, letter width and x-height). Picking one changes the font of every
    text box that used the missing one, as one undo step.
  - **Locate fonts…** opens your computer's font folder; a font you add there is picked up when you come back to the
    window.
  - **Remember for this document** keeps the choice, so the bar does not ask again.
- **Fonts travel with your file.** When you save, any installed font the page uses whose licence allows it is carried
  inside the `.quad`, so whoever opens it sees the real font. Bundled fonts are never carried, since every copy of
  quadrule has them. An **Embed fonts** tick in the Page tab turns this off. Fonts whose licence allows only viewing
  travel as saved outlines instead.
- **Missing characters** can now come from any font installed on your computer, after the chosen font and the bundled
  fonts.
- **Two new linter rules.** **L11** marks text drawn from saved outlines because its font is missing. **L20** lists
  the installed fonts a page uses and whether each travels with the file. On Save, Save As and Export, a short sheet
  says the same, with a "Don't warn again for this document" tick. Autosave never asks.

## New in rung 12b: turning patterns

- **Pattern regions turn**, like every other object: drag the round handle above the region or type an angle in the
  Rotation field. It catches at 0, 90, 180 and 270°; Shift gives 15° steps. The frame and its lines turn together and
  lines are cut at the turned frame. A group holding a pattern region can turn too.
- **Pattern angle** turns the lines inside a region's frame, which stays put. Find it in the Style tab beside the
  spacing. A frame's turn and its pattern angle add, so a frame turned 20° with a pattern angle of −20° keeps its lines
  level.
- Lines at a quarter turn stay sharp. At other angles they cannot sit on whole pixels and look softer on the tablet;
  the linter's **L02** says so.

## Files

- Files are saved as format **0.13.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–12)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), from one design, in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff), turned whole or with their lines turned inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts are missing open from saved outlines; fonts whose licence allows it travel inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, typing on the page,
  unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with twelve rules.

Source: `v0.12.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.11.0`.
