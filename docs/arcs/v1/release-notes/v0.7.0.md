Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,

## New in rung 7a: groups

- **Group and ungroup:** select two or more objects and press Ctrl+G (Ctrl+Shift+G to ungroup), or use the Group and
  Ungroup buttons in the Arrange tab and the layers panel. Groups can hold groups, as deep as you like.
- **Working inside a group:** a click picks the whole group; double-click to go inside and pick one object; Escape
  steps back out. Ctrl+click picks the innermost object straight away. The group you are inside has a faint dashed
  outline.
- **Moving, resizing and turning:** a group drags, nudges, snaps, duplicates and copies as one object. Resizing a
  group scales everything in it. A group turns with its own handle, and objects already turned inside it keep their
  angles on top. A group holding text or a pattern does not turn, since those do not rotate yet.
- **Constraints work as in Figma:** objects in a group keep their own constraints against the page, so grouping never
  changes how a page looks on the other device.
- **Opacity:** a group's opacity fades it as one, so overlapping objects inside do not show through each other.
- **Layers panel:** now a tree with fold arrows; drag rows into and out of groups.
- File format 0.7.0: files saved by earlier versions open unchanged and export the same pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–7a)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872).
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes, and
  pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid) bound to the page's rhythm; groups of any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with seven rules.

Source: `v0.7.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.6.1`.
