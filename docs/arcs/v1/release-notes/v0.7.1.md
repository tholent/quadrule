## New in rung 7b: align and distribute

- **Align:** select objects and use the six align buttons in the Arrange tab (left, centre, right, top, middle,
  bottom). **Align to** chooses what they line up against: the selection, the page or the page's margins. With one
  object picked it starts on Page, with several on Selection, and each remembers what you choose.
- **Distribute:** with three or more objects, space them evenly across or down, by equal gaps or by equal distances
  between their centres.
- **Crisp results:** with the pixel snap on, aligned edges land on whole device pixels, and equal gaps are whole
  pixels (any spare pixel goes to the first gaps).
- Turned objects and groups align by the box round their turned corners; locked objects stay put. Each command is one
  undo step.
- **Mixed values:** with several objects selected, the Arrange tab's X, Y, Width, Height and Rotation read "mixed"
  where they differ; typing a value sets it on all of them.
- No change to the file format or to exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–7)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872).
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes, and
  pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid) bound to the page's rhythm; groups of any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and
  crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with seven rules.

Source: `v0.7.1`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.7.0`.
