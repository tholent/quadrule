**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
lets you make and delete layers, approved by chris on 2026-11-18. The starters come in `v0.16.3`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New: make and delete layers

- **New layer** and **Delete layer** are in the Layers panel's button row, and on a layer's right-click menu.
- **A new layer** goes just above the picked one (or on top), named "Layer 2", "Layer 3"…, with its name ready to type
  over.
- **Deleting a layer** that still has objects on it asks first; an empty one goes at once. Undo brings it back either
  way.
- The last layer, a locked layer, and a layer holding locked objects can't be deleted; the button says why.
- Dragging an object's row onto another layer moves it there, as before.

## Fixed

- **Renaming in the Layers panel:** Enter and clicking away now close the name field, and Esc cancels it, for layers
  and objects alike.

## Files

- No change to the `.quad` format (still **0.17.0**); exported pixels are unchanged.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a, borders and layers)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff) with optional borders and rounded corners, turned whole or with their lines turned inside; groups and
  live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.16.2`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.16.1`.
