**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
marks rung 16a, the welcome screen and the gallery, approved by chris on 2026-11-18. The starters themselves come in
`v0.16.1`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New in rung 16a: the welcome screen

- **A welcome screen** in place of the empty start. Down the left: **Blank page** (the New dialog), **Open…** and your
  **Recent** files. On the right: the gallery, with tabs for All, Paper, Notes, Planning, Bullet journal, Creative and
  My templates, a **Device** picker and a **Search** box. It shows when quadrule starts without a file, after File ▸
  Close, and for File ▸ New… over an open page (Esc goes back). Dropping a `.quad` on it opens it.
- **Pictures drawn for your device.** Each tile is drawn for the device you pick, in the background, so the window
  stays quick while they come in. A page that does not target that device is drawn on its own and says which.
- **My templates.** File ▸ **Save as template…** keeps a copy of the page in the app's data folder. Picking a template
  opens an untitled copy, so the template itself never changes; right-click to remove one or show the folder.
- **Starters are coming.** The Paper, Notes, Planning, Bullet journal and Creative tabs say their starters are coming;
  they arrive in `v0.16.1`.
- **Recent files**, the last ten, on the welcome screen and in File ▸ Open Recent. A file that has gone missing shows
  greyed, and you can take it off the list.

## A File menu, and one Export

- **File** now holds New… (Ctrl+N), Open… (Ctrl+O), Open Recent, Save (Ctrl+S), Save As… (Ctrl+Shift+S), Save as
  template…, Export… (Ctrl+E) and Close (Ctrl+W); Cmd on a Mac. The toolbar keeps the tools and view controls.
- **Export… is one item now.** Ctrl+E opens the Export sheet with your last choices filled in and its Export button
  ready, so **Ctrl+E then Enter** repeats your last export. Export again and Ctrl+Shift+E are gone.

## Files

- No change to the `.quad` format (still **0.16.0**); exported pixels are unchanged.
- Your recent files are kept in `settings.ron` in the app's config folder, and your templates in `templates/` in its
  data folder (`~/.local/share/quadrule/templates` on Linux).

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff), turned whole or with their lines turned inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, typing on the page,
  unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.16.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.15.0`.
