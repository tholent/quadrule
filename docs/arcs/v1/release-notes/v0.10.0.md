## New in rung 10: the repeat tool

### Repeats

- Select something and press **Repeat** (beside Group and Ungroup) to repeat it. Several selected objects are grouped
  first. A repeat is **live**: change the source and every copy changes with it.
- **Linear** (across or down) or **Grid** (columns × rows).
- **Count** gives a fixed number of copies. **Fill** draws as many as fit in the repeat's box. Pin that box to the
  margins and each tablet gets as many as fit on its own page: more on the Manta, fewer on the Nomad.
- **Spacing** is a pitch (middle to middle) or a gap (edge to edge), in any unit. The default, **Auto**, is the
  source's size rounded up to whole rhythm units, so copies sit side by side on the rhythm and never overlap.
- **Start** or **Centre** decides where spare space in a Fill box goes.
- Every copy is the source moved by whole device pixels, so each one looks the same on the tablet.

### Alternation

- Rules count **columns**, **rows** or **cells** (or copies, in a linear repeat): "every 7th", or a list such as
  "3, 7". A rule can **skip** those copies or give them another **line role** or **fill role**. A habit tracker with
  a heavier line every seventh day is one repeat with one rule.

### Editing

- Click any copy to select the repeat. Double-click any copy to edit the source in place; Escape leaves.
- The **Repeat** tab in the inspector holds every setting and the rules.
- **Expand** turns a repeat into one group of ordinary objects.
- A repeat can hold a group, sit inside a group, or repeat another repeat (up to 5,000 copies).
- Other objects snap to the copies' edges and corners.

### Linter

- **L06** (safe zones) and **L10** (off the page) check every copy, with one finding per repeat that says how many.
- **L16** (off-rhythm) and **L17** (mixed rhythm) check a repeat's spacing.

### Speed

- The graphics-chip canvas (View → Canvas) now redraws only what changed. On a Raspberry Pi 5, drawing on a busy page
  went from about 50 ms to 24 ms a frame, and dragging on the pattern page from 34 ms to 17 ms.

### Files

- Files are saved as format **0.10.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–10)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), from one design, in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes, and
  pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell, music staff), bound to the page's
  rectangular or isometric rhythm or spaced on their own; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and
  crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with nine rules.
