**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
lets a pattern region choose where its major lines start, lets right-aligned text end on the rhythm, and adds a
Homebrew cask for macOS, approved by chris on 2026-11-23. The starters come in `v0.16.7`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

On a Mac with Homebrew, `brew install --cask tholent/tap/quadrule` installs the same app as the `.dmg`, once the tap
has been filled in for this release.

## New

- **Where major lines start.** A pattern region with major lines has two new rows in its settings, **Major rows from**
  and **Major columns from**, each **Top**, **Centre** or **Rhythm origin**, so rows can stay on the page's rhythm
  while columns are centred, or the other way round.
  - **Top** makes the region's first line major (its top line for rows, its left line for columns), then every Nth.
  - **Centre** makes the region's middle line major, and the other majors go off every Nth line either way from it.
    With an even number of lines, the upper (or left) of the two middle lines is the major one.
  - **Rhythm origin** counts from the page's rhythm origin, as every region did before.
- New regions start at **Top**. Isometric regions offer **Centre** and **Rhythm origin**, and their three families'
  majors meet on the middle. Cornell counts its ruled lines from the region's top, and a ruling turned inside its
  frame counts in its own direction.
- **Homebrew.** macOS users can install and update quadrule with Homebrew from tholent's shared tap (see Download).

## Changed

- **L16** (off the rhythm) now accepts a **right-aligned** text box whose right edge is on a rhythm line; other text
  and objects are still judged by their left edge.

## Files

- The `.quad` format is now **0.20.0**: files from earlier versions open unchanged and keep counting their major lines
  from the rhythm origin, so they export the same bytes. A file whose regions count from Top or Centre needs this
  version.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a, borders, layers, fixes batches 1 and 2, the starter fixes, calendars and major lines from)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell, music
  staff and grand staff, calendar) with optional borders and rounded corners, major lines counted from where you
  choose, turned whole or with their lines turned inside, and mini calendars; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, smooth zoom, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery; menus on every system.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.16.6`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.16.5`.
