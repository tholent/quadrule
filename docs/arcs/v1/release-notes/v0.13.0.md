**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
marks rung 13 (13a the profile system and 13b paper sizes, shipped together), both approved by chris on 2026-11-17.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New in rung 13a: your own profiles

- **The Profiles window** (the **Profiles…** button in the toolbar or beside the device ticks in the Page tab) lists
  the built-in screens and your own. Each has a form: name, size, PPI, margins, and folding sections for line roles,
  fill roles, gray levels and pen grays, safe zones (a table of pixel boxes) and notes. The open page redraws as you
  change a value; **Save** keeps it, **Cancel** throws it away.
- **Tune the Manta or Nomad.** Changing a built-in screen's values makes a local copy that every page on this computer
  uses, marked **Edited** wherever the device's name shows. Its size, PPI and short name stay locked, so it is still
  the same device. **Reset to built-in** brings the shipped values back.
- **Make your own.** **New…** copies any profile as a starting point (marked Unverified) and lets you change
  everything, size and PPI included; **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **Import…** and **Export…** (a `.ron` file) do what
  they say. Your profiles live in quadrule's config folder (on Linux `~/.config/quadrule/profiles/`).
- **Profiles travel with your file.** Saving puts a copy of every profile the page uses that is not a plain built-in
  inside the `.quad`. Opening it on a computer that lacks the profile uses that copy, with a bar offering **Add to my
  profiles**. When an incoming profile has the same name as one of yours, you choose **Merge** (its values update
  yours), **Rename** (keep it as a separate profile) or **Not now**.
- **A broken profile file never stops the app.** It is skipped and listed in the Profiles window with its error.

## New in rung 13b: paper sizes

- **Eleven paper sizes** for printing: ISO A3, A4, A5, A6 and B5, JIS B5, US Letter, US Legal, Tabloid, Half Letter
  and Executive. They sit in a **Paper** group in New…, the Page tab and the Profiles window, and work as the main
  target or alongside a tablet.
- **Choose the resolution per page:** 150, 200, 300 (default) or 600 PPI, beside the paper's tick in the Page tab. An
  A4 page at 300 PPI exports as 2480 × 3508 pixels.
- Lines keep the Nomad's weights by physical size, so a page looks the same weight on paper as on the tablet. These
  values are not yet checked on a printer (Unverified).
- A landscape paper page exports as a **wide** PNG, ready to print sideways.
- The safe-zone checks are skipped on paper, which has no toolbars.
- Known: on the export-pixels canvas, very large pages (Tabloid or A3 at 600 PPI) are slow to drag and draw; the
  graphics-chip canvas (View → Canvas) is fast. A fix is planned before v1.0.

## Files

- Files are saved as format **0.14.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–13)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design,
  in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff), turned whole or with their lines turned inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, typing on the page,
  unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, and a
  linter with twelve rules.
