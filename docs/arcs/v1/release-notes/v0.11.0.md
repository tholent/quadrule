**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
marks rung 11 (11a fonts and 11b the text box, shipped together), approved by chris on 2026-11-15.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New in rung 11: text and fonts

### Fonts

- **Bundled fonts**, all under the SIL Open Font License, with their licence texts in every package: Atkinson
  Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, Inter, Literata, Caveat (handwritten) and Noto Emoji. Each has its
  italic where the font has one. The packages are about 6 MB bigger.
- **Italic** is a new Style setting on a text box. A font with no italic stays upright, and the setting says so.
- **Weights** follow each font's own range. Inter and Literata also change their letter shapes with size, as their
  designers intended.
- **Your installed fonts** can be used too, on Linux, macOS and Windows. They are read again in the background when you
  come back to the window, so a font you just installed appears without a restart.
- **The font picker** in the Text tab lists Bundled then System fonts, each name in its own font, with a search box and
  a preview at the tablet's real size.
- A character the chosen font lacks is taken from the bundled fonts only (emoji from Noto Emoji), so a page looks the
  same on every computer. Filling gaps from installed fonts comes later, with a warning when it happens.

### Emoji

- An **Emoji** button (toolbar and Text tab) opens a picker with search by name and nine groups. Emoji are drawn in
  black and white from Noto Emoji, crisp on e-ink. A pick goes into the text you are typing, or into a new text box in
  the middle of the view.

### The text box

- **Type on the page:** double-click a text box to type in it, with a cursor, selection, and copy and paste. A new
  text box starts ready to type.
- **Auto height or Fixed:** an auto box grows with its text; a fixed box keeps its size. Pull a box's top or bottom
  edge to make it fixed.
- **Overflow:** when a fixed box holds more than fits, the lines that don't fit are left out, a red **+** marks the box
  on screen (never in the export), and the linter's **L08** says how many lines are hidden on which tablet.
- **Paragraph spacing**, and **vertical alignment** (top, middle, bottom) in fixed boxes.
- **Align baselines to rhythm:** every line of text stands on the page's ruled lines, the way handwriting sits on
  ruled paper. A tick, "Small gap above the line", lifts the letters 0.3 mm clear of the rule.
- **Rotation:** text turns like shapes, with the rotate handle or the Rotation field. A group holding text can turn too.

### Speed

- On the graphics-chip canvas (View → Canvas), typing now redraws only the line that changed. On a Raspberry Pi 5,
  typing on a text-heavy page went from 82 ms to about 9 ms a frame, and dragging from about 17 ms to 4 ms.

### Files

- Files are saved as format **0.12.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–11)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), from one design, in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff), bound to the page's rectangular or isometric rhythm or spaced on their own; groups and live repeats of
  any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, typing on the page,
  unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with ten rules.

Source: `v0.11.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.10.0`.
