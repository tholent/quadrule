**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
marks rung 14 (14a the new lint rules and 14b the lint window and preflight, shipped together), both approved by chris
on 2026-11-17.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New in rung 14a: six more checks, from measurements on the tablet

- **L03, gray too light:** a line, dot, fill or text so pale it may not show on the device. The limits come from the
  calibration sheets: a 1 px line lighter than 187, a 2 px or wider line lighter than 204, square dots by size (2 px
  lighter than 102, 3 px than 170, 4 px than 187), fills lighter than 204, text lighter than 153.
- **L05, gray off the clean levels:** a gray the screen does not draw flat, so it may band or dither.
- **L07, text too small:** under 6 pt, the smallest size tested on the device.
- **L09, too tight to write in** (templates only): a rhythm or ruled spacing under 5 mm.
- **L13, large dark fills** (templates only): fills at gray 102 or darker covering more than a tenth of the page,
  which can ghost or slow the screen.
- **L14, soft thin lines:** a stroke 2 px or thinner that is slanted or curved, so most of its pixels come out as
  in-between grays. (The "diagonal line" note moved here from L02.)
- **The gray judged is the gray on the page**, after opacity, for role and custom styles alike.
- **Every limit lives in the device's profile**, in a new **Lint checks** section of the Profiles window, editable
  with Reset to built-in. A limit left empty skips its check, and the Lint tab says which checks were skipped on each
  device and why. Paper sizes run only the text-size and handwriting checks.

## New in rung 14b: fixing what lint finds

- **The Lint tab** has a **Findings for** picker (all devices, or one), and a fix button on each finding that has one:
  **Snap to pixel grid**, **Use nearest clean gray**, **Use <Role> role**, **Move inside safe area**, **Use 6 pt**,
  **Snap to rhythm** (one object, or all of them), **Bind to rhythm** and **Center rhythm origin**. Each fix is one
  undo step.
- **Ignore this rule for this document** turns a check off for this page on every device; **Ignored rules** at the end
  of the Lint tab turns it back on. It is saved in the file.
- **Free placement**, a checkbox in the Arrange tab, lets an object sit off the rhythm on purpose without a warning.
- **Before you export**, a sheet lists the warnings by device, with **Jump to object**, the fixes and **Ignore**; notes
  are folded away; then **Cancel** or **Export anyway**. The installed-fonts warning is part of this sheet now; Save
  and Save As keep their own small font sheet.
- **Gray swatches** in the Style tab: the device's clean grays in two rows, **Show all grays** for the full slider, a
  **matches <Role>** badge when a custom style equals a role, and **Use recommended** when it does not. Fill and text
  grays have them too.
- The Page tab keeps a paper size's PPI choice on one line.

## Files

- Files are saved as format **0.15.0** (the profile's lint limits, ignored rules, Free placement). Every older file
  opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–14)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design,
  in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff), turned whole or with their lines turned inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, typing on the page,
  unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.
