**Pre-alpha.** quadrule is not ready for everyday use yet; files made now may need upgrading later. This release
brings the first batch of fixes asked for before the starters: smoother zoom, grand staves spaced on their own, and
icons for the layer buttons, approved by chris on 2026-11-20. Menus for each system come in `v0.16.4`, Quick Look in
`v0.16.5`, the starters in `v0.16.6`.

## Download

Installers for Linux (AppImage and `.deb`, x86-64 and ARM64), macOS (universal `.dmg`, unsigned) and Windows (`.msi`,
unsigned) are attached below. macOS and Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown developer; `README.md` says
how to open it anyway. Or build from source (`DEVELOPMENT.md`).

## New

- **Smooth zoom.** A trackpad pinch and Ctrl+scroll now follow your fingers instead of jumping a whole step, and keep
  the point under the pointer still. Passing close to 1:1 or to Fit settles exactly on it. The + and − buttons and
  their keys still move in clean steps, from 1/8 to 16×.
- **Space between grand staves.** With Grand staff on, a music staff's count is now the space from the bottom of one
  grand staff to the top of the next, in rhythm units, so grand staves never overlap and changing the line gap keeps
  the spacing. A single staff keeps **Staff every**, top to top, as before. Grand staves made before this release
  look and export exactly as they did.
- **Layer icons.** The Layers panel's seven buttons are line icons on one row, in three groups (Move up, Move down;
  New layer, Delete layer; Group, Ungroup, Repeat). Hovering shows the name, then what it does, or why it is greyed.

## Changed

- **Exports are the same on every computer.** quadrule now draws every export with plain arithmetic on every
  processor, so two computers can no longer differ by one gray level on a curved or turned edge. Pages with curves or
  turned lines may export a few edge pixels one level apart from `v0.16.2`; nothing else moves.

## Files

- The `.quad` format is now **0.18.0** (it adds the space between grand staves). Older files open unchanged; a page
  saved by this version, opened in `v0.16.2`, spaces its grand staves the older way.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–16a, borders, layers and fixes batch 1)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; start from a welcome screen with recent files and your own templates; export
  exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and Nomad (1404 × 1872), for eleven paper sizes at
  a chosen PPI, and for screens you describe yourself, from one design, in portrait or landscape, named and foldered
  the way you choose.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes with
  bundled and installed fonts, emoji, and pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid, isometric, hex, Cornell,
  music staff and grand staff) with optional borders and rounded corners, turned whole or with their lines turned
  inside; groups and live repeats of any of them.
- Pages whose fonts or profiles are missing still open; fonts whose licence allows it, and your own profiles, travel
  inside the file.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers (made, named and deleted),
  typing on the page, smooth zoom, unlimited undo, autosave and crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices and editable in the Profiles window, the safe-zone overlay, a
  linter with eighteen rules and quick fixes, and a check before export.

Source: `v0.16.3`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.16.2`.
