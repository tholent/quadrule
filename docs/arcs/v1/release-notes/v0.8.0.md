## New in rung 8: several devices and landscape pages

### One page, both tablets

- **The primary device and the others.** A page is designed on its primary device; every other device it targets
  follows through each object's constraints.
- **Per-device changes (overrides).** With another device on screen, moving, resizing, nudging, changing a font size or
  hiding an object changes it on that device only. Every other edit (style, fill, text, rotation, constraints) changes
  the design on all devices, and the status bar says so.
- **Groups.** A group moved or resized on another device gets one override, its box there; what is inside is scaled
  into it.
- **Seeing what changed.** A small dot marks a changed value and a changed layer row. The **Overrides** tab lists what
  differs on the device on screen, with a Reset beside each and "Reset all on this device".
- **The previews strip.** Along the bottom of the canvas, a small live picture of each device's page; the one on screen
  is outlined. Click a picture to switch to that device, or fold the strip shut.
- **The Page tab.** Tick devices in and out, and use **Make primary** to make another device's layout the design
  without moving a pixel on any device.
- **Lint L15** notes where constraints alone put an object off the page, on top of another or squeezed thin on another
  device.
- Align and distribute, nudges and group drags now work on the device on screen.

### Landscape pages

- Choose **Portrait** or **Landscape** in New… or in the Page tab (one undo step; objects follow their own
  constraints onto the turned page).
- A landscape page is designed upright on screen and exported **turned a quarter turn clockwise** into the tablet's
  ordinary portrait PNG, the design's top along the tablet's right edge. Read it in portrait mode, holding the tablet
  with its right edge up. The turn is exact: no pixel is resampled.
- The tablet's toolbars and footers are shown, snapped to and linted where they fall on the turned page, and New…'s
  margins clear them.
- Tried on the Nomad; the Manta is expected to behave the same but has not been tried.

### Files

- Files are saved as format **0.8.0**. Every older file opens with the same exported pixels.

## What quadrule can do so far (rungs 1–8)

- Open, edit and save `.quad` files; export exact-pixel 8-bit gray PNGs for the Supernote Manta (1920 × 2560) and
  Nomad (1404 × 1872), from one design, in portrait or landscape.
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars (fill, stroke, dashes, caps, rotation, opacity), text boxes, and
  pattern regions (ruled lines, dots, square grid) bound to the page's rhythm; groups of any of them.
- Select, move, resize, marquee, duplicate, copy and paste, align and distribute, layers, unlimited undo, autosave and
  crash recovery.
- Snapping to the rhythm, margins, safe zones, the pixel grid, other objects' points and edges, and equal spacing.
- Line roles with values calibrated on real devices, the safe-zone overlay, and a linter with eight rules.

Source: `v0.8.0`. Earlier rungs are tagged `v0.1.0` to `v0.7.1`.
